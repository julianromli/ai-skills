# Final refinement of the current project

Use this checklist for the second prompt, together with `style-guide.md` and the bundled external references. It applies to the current user's latest version. Revisions must preserve successful and approved decisions; the absence of a technique that a scene does not need is not itself a defect.

## Preserve the foundation

- Preserve the previous version; make changes in a new working version with clean layers.
- Preserve the approved meaning, speech, scene order, duration, pacing, text and successful motion. If only the picture changes and the timeline is identical, retain the approved audio track without a new mix.
- Treat inserts explicitly marked “keep” by the user in words or screenshots as approved. Match screenshots to the current timeline. Do not transfer lists of retained scenes from other projects.
- Work with the original picture, text and treatment. Do not overlay a second set of captions or new grain on top of old versions already baked in. If necessary clean layers are unavailable, state exactly which asset is missing.
- Record a short list of actual defects with current timecodes. Do not turn final refinement into an unjustified redesign of the entire video.

## 1. Insert selection

- Replace unmarked inserts that look random, generic, glossy or out of place in the overall archival system. Preserve user-specified exceptions.
- Each new insert connects to a specific thought and topic. Analogue equipment suits technology; for another topic, find its own materials. Do not impose computers and laboratories on every story.
- Align the palette, physical texture, image softness and compositional character: cream and charcoal, occasional ochre and muted red; photography, real details and convincing collage.
- Remove repetitive uses of the same subject disguised as new scenes through cropping, mirroring and recolouring. Vary the main subject, shot size and composition while preserving connecting motifs.
- Preserve the truthfulness of interfaces, names, numbers and product demonstrations. Generations remain illustrations.
- New Seedance 2.5 generations are optional and are performed only with access and agreed spending. Without access, use suitable available materials; do not silently substitute the model.
- Update the actual sources, licenses and on-screen attributions. An old credit must not remain on a new photograph or extend beyond the display time of the source it refers to.

## 2. Grain, bloom, exposure and B&W

- Check grain in motion: fresh on every frame, fine, light, predominantly monochrome, with natural tonal behaviour. Remove the appearance of a pasted-on or translated noise image, large blotches and chromatic dirt.
- Account for grain baked into the source. Avoid doubled noise and attempts to hide defects with excessive treatment.
- Adjust bloom before final grain: soft highlights that preserve shape and detail. Remove a murky veil, light rings and visible orange/red letter outlines. Control halation separately from bloom and grain.
- Check neutral B&W, thin white lettering and the outgoing shot throughout the entire transition. A good static final frame does not prove that the transition is clean.
- Keep B-roll treatment within its actual region. In a moving split, the treatment mask follows the panel; the presenter's skin and important interfaces remain clean.
- Make B&W bright and soft, preserving controlled blacks, open midtones and smooth highlights. Correct crushed shadows, grey haze, colour casts and excessive darkening. Correct each source separately.
- Dust, scratches, burns and visible flicker are not added as mandatory signs of an archival look.

## 3. Text, meaning and timing of motion

- Ensure that authored typography uses exactly three roles and no more than three families: sans serif, editorial serif with italics, and monospace. Check the families and styles actually rendered, not just the fallback list.
- Check words against speech, names, numbers, line breaks, safe margins and legibility in a reduced-size preview. Remove stretched letters, cropped meaningful elements and conflicts between subtitles and emphasis words.
- Preserve successful soft, active animation: a meaningful entrance, sufficient reading time and a neat exit. Fix accidental jumps, excessive bounce, blur and restless movement.
- For every text element crossing a cut, check that the same thought continues. Preserve its position and continuous animation clock. Completed lines must not linger over new speech or subsequent captions.
- Preserve selective stepped motion in text transformations. A working guide is around 10–15 updates/s; 12.5 for a 25 fps master is a setting, not a measurement of the reference. Do not lower the rate of the entire video, masks, grain or audio.
- If the scene start has shifted, recalculate relative caption times while preserving their required absolute alignment with speech.

## 4. Masks and depth

- Check occlusions in the order background → text → real foreground, without artificial visibility of letters through the body.
- For each masked word, verify the source, clip time, crop, scale, position and flip. Recalculate the mask after changing the insert or framing.
- Check hair, glasses, hands and clothing edges in consecutive frames. Fix jitter, halos, missing parts of the figure and mismatches with motion.
- If occlusion is excessive, change the text's placement, size and timing while preserving its meaning. The number of visible pixels does not replace checking whether the word can be read.
- If a high-quality mask is unattainable, use a clean, readable composition without it. Do not hide a defect with a rectangular patch or translucent letters on the face.

## 5. Presenter split entry

- Apply the new side entry only when a presenter and an insert are present simultaneously. Full-screen B-roll and splits made only of inserts do not automatically receive this motion.
- The panel enters softly from the left or right, shifting and reframing the previous picture. The starting guide is 0.3–0.45 s; tuning follows the actual rhythm, not a mechanical template.
- Check facial proportions, free space around the face and framing continuity. Remove black gaps, stretching, an abrupt final jump, flashes, bounce and unexpected zooms.
- Text, crop, grain and bloom move with their panel. Check every phase, including the outgoing shot.
- Preserve existing transitions explicitly approved by the user as exceptions; do not replace them without a reason.

## 6. Final acceptance

1. Export a new version and check that exact file. Do not use a previous report as confirmation of its quality.
2. Verify the current duration, format, aspect ratio, pixel aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate and frame count, colour accuracy and complete decoding. Use the current project's parameters.
3. Check for missing media, accidental black frames, unintentional freezes, unwanted bars, stretching and technical artifacts. An intentional hold on a photograph is acceptable.
4. Check internal source edits: a few frames of an adjacent take must not appear in a presenter segment. Ensure that inserts cover the required time and have sufficient source duration, accounting for speed.
5. Watch the entire final MP4 at normal speed and listen to the sound. Check synchronization, speech intelligibility, the absence of new audio defects, exposure, grain, bloom and text motion.
6. Separately review changed transitions, all occlusions and thin lettering in consecutive frames and at reduced size. A contact sheet and metrics do not replace checking motion and sound.
7. Fix discovered defects and repeat the relevant checks. A fixed number of iterations is not a completion criterion.
8. Record viewing, listening and technical checks actually performed separately. If a check or export is impossible, state the specific limitation; do not declare an unverified result a checked final version.

## Delivery

- The final MP4 and a version without added music if music was used.
- An updated editable project, assets or a clear list of external dependencies, current sources and attributions.
- A short list of specific changes and completed checks, with any remaining limitations.
- Portable style settings kept separate from this video's timecodes, people, assets and specific exceptions.

When the necessary tools are available, perform the edit and export itself rather than stopping at recommendations.
